About FlyEasy

Founded by Shanmukeswara Reddy Medapati

I started FlyEasy with a simple dream — to make air travel affordable, comfortable, and trustworthy for everyone.

Coming from a modest background, I always believed that traveling should not be a luxury only for a few people.

As a student of technology and innovation, I learned how digital systems can improve real-life experiences.

Today, FlyEasy is my promise to travelers that they deserve safety, fairness, and respect.

— Shanmukeswara Reddy Medapati, Founder & CEO
